## Key takeaways

1. **Content is the differentiator we can win.** F&S Hauling (the competitor) wins 458 keywords almost entirely through blog content — driveway cost guides, yardage calculators, axle comparisons, material pages. The client has 0 clicks on every blog post. Beating this content engine is the Tier 2 plan.
2. **Map-pack is won by GBP, not website.** Solid Rock has 44 organic keywords but #1 local pack. Our Phase 2 GBP work (complete profile, reviews, photos) is as important as the site content. They reinforce each other.
3. **Aggregators own "gravel delivery" organic.** We cannot outrank Hello Gravel / Gravel Monkey on generic queries. Strategy: (a) win long-tail local queries ("gravel delivery Shelby NC clay soil"), (b) get listed on the aggregators themselves for citation + referral traffic.
4. **Brand collision is urgent and unambiguous.** "f&s hauling" = 118 GSC impressions aimed at a different company. Old blog slugs/titles used "F&S Hauling" and must be cleaned (Phase 1 fixed the 404 slug; remaining slugs/titles are Tier 3 cleanup). Never use "F&S Hauling" as the client's name in any new content.
5. **Local rivals are weak online.** Most have Facebook-only or no websites. A complete, specific, locally true site can outrank them on organic for long-tail queries even with low authority.
6. **"dirt hauling" is already winnable.** The client ranked #1 organic for "dirt hauling" in a Shelby-located SERP with title "Dump Truck Services Shelby NC". Proof the market responds to a clear, locally relevant page.

## What NOT to do

- Do not try to out-rank aggregators on generic "gravel delivery" — win the long-tail local queries instead.
- Do not publish thin city-page spam — only pages with unique, locally true content. (The "50+ cities served" claim is unsupported by the code; do not repeat it.)
- Do not use "F&S Hauling" as the client's name anywhere.
- Do not invent competitor data; all figures above come from the prompt's researched competitor table (2026-10-22).
